# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 4, 2026

QuicLoc ("we," "our," or "us") operates the QuicLoc Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information remains secure.

### Data Collection and Use

**QuicLoc does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal user data with the developer or any third party.**

*   **No Analytics:** We do not use any third-party analytics or crash reporting services.
*   **No Servers:** QuicLoc operates entirely on your local device. We do not have any servers that receive, process, or store your location or contact information.
*   **Local Processing:** All sensitive data, including your whitelist of trusted contacts and location information, is managed exclusively on your device. Contact data is stored using AES-256-GCM encryption backed by the Android Keystore.
*   **Permissions:** QuicLoc requires certain permissions to function (e.g., SMS, Location, Notification Access). These are used strictly to provide the app's core functionality, such as responding to location requests from trusted contacts. This information is never transmitted to us or any third party.

### Third-Party Services

QuicLoc does not use any third-party services or SDKs that collect data on your behalf.

### Your Choices

Since QuicLoc does not collect any data, there is no user data for you to manage, delete, or request from us. You have full control over the application's data and functionality through the app settings and standard Android permission controls.

### Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please reach out via the contact information provided on the [QuicLoc GitHub repository](https://github.com/your-username/quicloc).

### Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
