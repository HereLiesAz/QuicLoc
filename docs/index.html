How It Works

1️⃣

You list who you trust

Pick them from your contacts. Anyone not on the list is ignored silently — they're never told the app exists.

2️⃣

They text you loc

Just that word, in a text message or in WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Messenger or Google Messages.

3️⃣

Your phone answers

A Google Maps link goes straight back to them, in seconds. No prompt, no sound, nothing for you to tap.

🚫

Then nothing happens

Your position isn't stored, streamed or logged — not on your phone, and not on any server, because there isn't one.

Set Up In Five Minutes

📥

Install & unlock

Grab the APK, open QuicLoc, unlock with your fingerprint or PIN.

✅

Grant texts & location

Each permission is explained before it's asked. Set location to "Allow all the time" — that's the step people miss.

👥

Add your people

One tap from your contacts list. Star up to three for the widget's safety-check alert.

🧪

Test it

Have one of them text you loc. The app's live checklist and diagnostics screen explain anything that doesn't work.
